# B2B Case Study: How a Marketing Agency Reduced Meeting Time by 35% Using Notion

**Word Count Target**: 1500 words  
**Readability Target**: Flesch-Kincaid 60+

## Executive Summary
A 20-person marketing agency struggled with scattered project information across Google Docs, emails, and Slack. By implementing Notion as a centralized workspace, the team reduced weekly meeting time by 35%, increased project delivery speed by 28%, and cut tool costs by 40% within 3 months.

## The Challenge
Before Notion, the agency faced 3 main problems:
1. **Information Silos**: Client briefs, assets, and tasks were spread across 5+ tools.
2. **Meeting Overload**: 6 hours per week spent in status meetings to track project updates.
3. **Onboarding Delays**: New hires took 2 weeks to understand where files and processes lived.

## The Solution
The agency adopted Notion and built 3 core systems:
1. **Client Hub Database**: One page per client with briefs, deadlines, and assets.
2. **Project Tracker**: Kanban board with task assignments, due dates, and status.
3. **Team Wiki**: Centralized SOPs, templates, and onboarding guides.

## Implementation
**Week 1**: Audit existing tools and migrate data to Notion databases.
**Week 2**: Build templates for client onboarding, content calendars, and meeting notes.
**Week 3**: Train team with 2 workshops. Set Notion as the single source of truth.
**Week 4**: Decommission 3 redundant tools and enforce Notion-first policy.

## Results
- **Metric 1**: 35% reduction in weekly meeting time, from 6 hours to 3.9 hours.
- **Metric 2**: 28% faster project delivery, average turnaround dropped from 14 to 10 days.
- **Metric 3**: 40% cost savings by replacing 3 paid tools with Notion.

## Conclusion
Centralizing workflows in Notion eliminated tool fatigue and improved transparency. The key to success was creating templates first, then training, then enforcing one workspace. This approach can be replicated by any small agency looking to scale.
