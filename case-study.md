# How Notion Replaced 5 Apps for a 10-Person Agency: Case Study

**Meta Description**: B2B case study showing how a 10-person content agency used Notion to cut tool costs by 60%, save 15 hours/week, and improve client delivery time.
**Keywords**: notion b2b case study, agency productivity, all in one workspace, notion for teams

## 1. Executive Summary
A 10-person content marketing agency was struggling with tool overload across 6 platforms. After implementing Notion as their central workspace, they reduced software costs by 60%, saved 15 hours per week, and cut client delivery time from 7 days to 4 days.

## 2. Company Background
**Client**: "ContentLab Agency" - 10 employees, 25 active clients
**Industry**: B2B Content Marketing
**Challenge**: Managing writers, editors, clients, and assets across Google Docs, Slack, Asana, Drive, and Email.

## 3. The Problem: Tool Overload and Lost Revenue
Before Notion, the team faced:
1.  **Context Switching**: Employees spent 2.5 hours/day switching between 6 apps
2.  **Lost Information**: Client feedback was buried in Slack threads and emails
3.  **High Costs**: $400/month on SaaS tools: Asana $99, Slack $80, Drive $60, Docs free, Trello $50, Grammarly $110
4.  **Missed Deadlines**: 4 missed client deadlines in Q1 due to poor visibility

Quote from CEO: "We were paying for productivity but getting chaos."

## 4. The Solution: Implementing Notion as All-in-One Workspace
After evaluating ClickUp, Coda, and Notion, they chose Notion for 3 reasons:
1.  **Databases**: Built a Client CRM, Content Calendar, and Task Tracker that all link together
2.  **Permissions**: Granular sharing so clients only see their projects
3.  **Templates**: Onboarding template reduced new client setup from 3 hours to 30 minutes

## 5. Implementation Process
The rollout took 2 weeks.
**Week 1**: Built 3 core databases: Clients, Content Pipeline, Team Tasks
**Week 2**: Migrated 200+ Google Docs. Trained team with 2 workshops. Created SOPs in Notion wiki.
**Key Feature**: Linked "Task" database to "Client" database so every task shows client name, deadline, and assets.

## 6. Measurable Results
After 60 days using Notion:

| Metric | Before Notion | After Notion | Improvement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS Tools Used | 6 | 2 | 66% Reduction |
| Monthly Software Cost | $400 | $160 | 60% Savings |
| Time Wasted on Switching | 15 hrs/week | 3 hrs/week | 80% Reduction |
| Avg Client Delivery Time | 7 days | 4 days | 43% Faster |
| Missed Deadlines | 4/quarter | 0 | 100% Reduction |

ROI: The agency saved $2,880/year on tools + ~$18,000/year in labor hours.

## 7. Challenges Faced
1.  **Learning Curve**: 2 team members took 1 week to adapt to databases
2.  **Migration**: Moving 200 docs manually took 3 days
3.  **Offline Access**: Notion mobile app was slower than Google Docs initially

How they solved it: Made video SOPs and assigned 1 "Notion Champion" for support.

## 8. Lessons Learned
1.  **Start with Templates**: Don't build from scratch. Duplicate and modify.
2.  **One Source of Truth**: If it's not in Notion, it doesn't exist. This rule was key.
3.  **Client Portals Work**: Sharing a read-only Notion page with clients reduced "status update" emails by 70%.

## 9. Conclusion
Notion didn't just replace apps. It became the operating system for the agency.
For B2B teams drowning in tools, consolidating into one workspace is the fastest way to cut costs and scale.
