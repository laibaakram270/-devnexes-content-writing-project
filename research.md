# Week 1 Research: Notion Case Study

## 1. Target Audience
**Primary**: Students, Freelancers, Small Teams, Project Managers
**Age**: 18-35
**Use Case**: Note-taking, task management, wikis, databases

## 2. Main Problem It Solves
"Tool overload" - People use 5-6 different apps for notes, tasks, docs, and wikis. 
Notion combines everything into 1 "all-in-one workspace" so you stop switching tabs.

## 3. Top 3 Competitors
1. **Google Docs/Drive** - Better for simple docs, but no databases or task management
2. **Trello** - Better for visual kanban boards, but weak on notes and docs
3. **Evernote** - Better for just note-taking, but not flexible or collaborative

## 4. Key Features to Highlight
1. Databases + Tables
2. Templates
3. Collaboration + Real-time editing
4. Customizable Pages
5. AI Writing Assistant
