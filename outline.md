# Week 2 Outline: Notion Case Study

## Title Options
1. How Notion Replaced 5 Apps and Saved Me 10 Hours a Week
2. From Chaos to Clarity: A Student's Journey with Notion
3. Why Small Teams Are Ditching Google Docs for Notion

## Target Reader
Busy students and freelancers aged 18-30 who juggle notes, tasks, and projects in different apps.

## Case Study Structure

### 1. Introduction - The Problem
Hook: "I was using Google Docs, Todoist, Evernote, and Drive... and still felt unorganized."
Explain the pain of "app switching" and tool overload.

### 2. Background
Who is the user? Ex: A freelance designer managing 8 clients.
What tools were they using before Notion?

### 3. The Solution: Why Notion
Introduce Notion as the "all-in-one workspace".
Key features that solved the problem: Databases, Templates, All-in-one

### 4. Implementation
How did they set it up? Step 1: Made a dashboard. Step 2: Created client databases.
Include 1 screenshot idea: "Notion Dashboard with tasks + notes"

### 5. Results
Before: 5 apps, wasted time, missed deadlines
After: 1 app, 10 hours saved per week, everything in one place
Use 1-2 numbers/stats to make it real.

### 6. Conclusion + CTA
Summary: Notion solved tool overload.
CTA: "Try Notion free with this template link"

## Keywords for SEO
notion case study, productivity tool, all in one workspace, notion for students
